## 1. Registry: the pool goes, every fee leaves (default)

1. **No sponsor pool.** Remove `claimSponsored`, `sponsor`, `pool`, `poolUsed`, `lastPoolDay`, `sponsoredVerified`,
   `POOL_DRAW_CAP`, `MAX_SPONSORED_VERIFIED`, `Pending.sponsored`, `_release`, `_burnOrRefund`, `_refund`, `PREVIEW_GAS`,
   `BURN_GAS`, `BURN_MIN`, `BURN_FEE_CAP`, the errors `InsufficientGas`, `BurnRefundFailed`, `PoolDrawTooLarge`,
   `PoolAlreadyUsed`, `PoolLifetimeLimit`, `PoolDailyLimit`, `NotMember`, `NotACatalogChainTrait`, the events `Sponsored`,
   `PoolSpent`, `BurnTriggered`, `BurnSkipped`, and every `payable`. The Registry never holds IMD at rest and never handles
   ETH. (Closes audit F-2, F-4, F-11, F-14, F-16 and the whole M-1 family of the first audit.) **No reference remains**
   (the owner, 2026-10-21): the feature leaves code, tests, documents and the site's copy, browser tests and config
   included. Acceptance, two greps over `src test script tools web README.md CATALOG.md CONCEPT.md INTEGRATION.md
   TEMPLATES.md`: `grep -rniE 'sponsor|donat'` prints nothing, and `grep -rniE '\bpool\b|pool\(\)|poolUsed|lastPoolDay|PoolSpent|PoolDraw'`
   prints nothing. Uniswap's own identifiers in the site's swap code and in vendored third-party ABIs (`poolManager`,
   `PoolKey`, `poolId`) do not match these patterns and stay; a comment or copy that calls the ETH/IMD market "a pool"
   is reworded. `DEPLOYMENTS.md`, `DISCLOSURE.md`, `audits/` and `docs/` keep the words as the record of the earlier
   deployments.
2. **Every fee leaves the registry.** `mintCard` moves `MINT_FEE` (0.1 IMD) and `registerSpec` moves `SPEC_FEE`
   (6.4 IMD) from the caller straight to BurnExecutor V2 (`0xe29386719C155B6847aD5a4E97C6674f10ffc750`) in the same
   transaction. The executor holds it until anyone bridges its balance to Base by paying the LayerZero fee
   (`previewBridge()` quotes the sendable amount and the fee; it differs from the raw balance by dust and the OFT's
   limits) and the Base receiver burns it. The registry promises the forward, not the burn: every text says "forwarded
   to the BurnExecutor for bridging and burning on Base", never "burned at once"; the executor's owner configuration,
   the bridge's delivery and the Base action are dependencies `DISCLOSURE.md` names (item 21). Oracle prices still go
   to the Intake. The constructor argument keeps its name `burnExecutor_`, must be nonzero and must have code (item 18).
   Tests: a mint raises the executor's IMD balance by exactly 0.1 IMD and leaves the registry's at zero; a registration
   by exactly 6.4 IMD; on a mainnet fork at a pinned block, `previewBridge()` after a mint reports a sendable amount
   that includes the forwarded fee up to the executor's limits.
3. **`abandon` on a shared fact is permissionless after `ABANDON_AFTER`** (audit F-3, the auditor's recommendation taken as
   written). The payer is no longer recorded; `Pending.payer` goes. A personal pair keeps its claimant-only `abandon`.
   Nothing is lost: the payer could already abandon after 31 days and still can, the fee was never refundable, and the
   stored-fact path is untouched; item 4 makes the 31 days a guarantee. Test: a stranger's `abandon` of a shared fact at
   day 15 reverts `TooEarly`, at day 32 succeeds and clears the pair; the claimant's personal abandon at day 32 succeeds;
   a stranger's personal abandon at day 32 reverts.
4. **The attestation's dates are bounded, and the boundary is strict** (audit F-7; cross audit CUT3-12). `_check` adds
   `a.issuedAt >= p.requestedAt`, `a.issuedAt <= p.requestedAt + ISSUE_WINDOW` with `ISSUE_WINDOW = 1 days` (the plane
   answers in minutes; a day is generous) and `a.expiresAt <= a.issuedAt + Composer.VALID_FOR_SECONDS`; a miss is
   reason 7. `abandon` requires `block.timestamp > p.requestedAt + ABANDON_AFTER` (strictly later; `TooEarly` at
   equality). With `ABANDON_AFTER = 31 days` the latest second an attestation can be valid is `requestedAt + 31 days`,
   when no abandon is possible yet, so a valid answer and an abandon can never both be possible at one second. Tests:
   an attestation issued before the request, one issued two days after it, and one valid for 31 days each miss with 7
   and clear the pair; one issued 23 hours after the request and valid for 30 days passes; at the exact second
   `requestedAt + 31 days` a delivery still verifies and `abandon` reverts `TooEarly`; one second later `abandon`
   succeeds and a delivery misses with 7 (both orderings tested).
5. **A pending record is never overwritten** (audit F-10). `_request` reverts `RequestIdTaken(requestId)` when
   `_pending[requestId].requestedAt != 0`. The Intake's request id and the plane's signed oracle id stay two different
   values (the first is the pending key, the second is stored). Tests: a mock Intake that returns a used id makes the
   whole `upgrade` revert, nothing pulled and nothing approved; an abandoned id followed by a successor request for the
   same pair: a late callback for the old id is reason 1 and the successor's reverse index is untouched.
6. **Trait 601 "Swarmed" is the operator fact, sworn, not read.** The family is renamed from "Armed" to "Swarmed" (the
   owner, 2026-10-21): in the Renderer's name table, the metadata, `CATALOG.md`, `TEMPLATES.md`, the site and every
   document; the live deployment keeps "Armed" as it is. See section 2 for the recipe. In the Registry: 601 leaves the
   direct set (`directSnapshot` covers the 13 Curator-read ids, bit 20 is never set, `hasDirectTrait(wallet, 601)` is
   false and `frozenDirect` keeps its layout); `hasTrait(wallet, 601)` answers from `_verified` like every oracle id;
   `_rule(601)` returns kind 6, `fromBlock` = 25,090,085 (the IdentityMD collection's creation block), a **living**
   `toBlock` = the request's `block.number - 64`, threshold 3; `_panelOf(6)` is 11 / 7 and `_answerTypeOf(6)` is bool.
   **The accepted window is kept, not recomputed** (cross audit CUT3-05): the pending record stores the window the body
   was composed with; `_check` compares the attestation's window with the stored one, never with `_rule` at the current
   head; on success the verified entry keeps that window, and `verified(claimant, 601)`, the oracle record in the
   metadata and every later read report it. A `Missed` 601 can be requested again at once (more jobs later). 601 is a
   paid upgrade like every oracle id. Tests: the window in the composed body equals the request block minus 64; an
   attestation for an earlier window of the same wallet misses with 6 or 10; a delivery after the head advanced by more
   than 64 blocks verifies against the stored window; `tokenURI` and `verified()` a day later print the stored window; a

7. **The IdentityMD dependency leaves the constructor.** `idmd_` and the `idmd` getter go from Registry and Composer

## 2. Composer: the operator recipe

10. **Kind 6, catalog-only.** A new question kind for id 601, not offered to community specs (`validateSpec` keeps
    kinds 1 to 5). Evidence class `panel` (archive state and logs across three contracts), panel 11 / quorum 7, answer
    type bool, `validForSeconds` as today.
11. **The question** (the exact wording is the builder's, the meaning is not): "Up to block `<TO>` on Ethereum mainnet,
    did the wallet `<A>` operate an IdentityMD seat through at least 3 accepted Identity.md swarm jobs while it held
    that seat? Answer true or false."
12. **`definitions`: eight keys, every value at most 512 characters** (the plane's limit, which the single-field method
    of the first draft exceeded at 1,676 characters: cross audit CUT3-03). The keys, in this order, with these texts
    (`<A>` the claimant in lowercase hex, `<TO>` the window's last block in decimal; the lengths below are measured
    with a 42-character address and an 8-digit block):
    - `method` (321): "Four steps, executed in order as one procedure: seats (step 1), agent (step 2), accepted
      (step 3), answer (step 4). Every read is of Ethereum mainnet archive logs and state up to block `<TO>` inclusive.
      A pruned, null or partial log read, or an endpoint without the window, is no evidence: write no answer, never
      false."
    - `seats` (509): "Step 1, seats. Every Transfer(from, to, tokenId; all indexed) log of the IdentityMD collection
      0x0000eC93127BAA929E58E97dd0095A2BFb38ec1D from block 25090085 to `<TO>` whose from or to is `<A>`. Per tokenId, a
      holding interval runs from the block of a transfer to the wallet (inclusive) to the block of the next transfer
      of that token away from it (exclusive); an interval still open at `<TO>` ends at `<TO>` inclusive. Same-block
      transfers are ordered by log index."
    - `agent` (429): "Step 2, the agents of a seat. The AgentBound(uint256 indexed agentId, uint8 indexed standard,
      address indexed tokenContract, uint256 tokenId, address registeredBy) logs of the adapter
      0xde152AfB7db5373F34876E1499fbD893A82dD336 with tokenContract equal to the collection and tokenId equal to the
      seat. An agent is bound to the seat from the block of its AgentBound log on. A seat without a binding has no
      agent and counts nothing."
    - `accepted` (493): "Step 3, accepted jobs. The NewFeedback logs (topic 0
      0x6a4a61743519c9d648a14e6493f47dbe3ff1aa29e7785c96c8326a205e58febc) of the reputation registry
      0x8004baa17c55a88189ae136b182e5fda19de9b63 whose topic 2 is the batcher 0xb6d0a187b050fa5bb0b87033a203f37becf4a775,
      whose second data word (bytes 32 to 63) equals 1, whose block lies inside one of the wallet's holding intervals
      of a seat, and whose topic 1 is an agent bound to that seat at or before the log's block. Each such log is one
      entry."
    - `answer` (216): "Step 4, the answer. true when the entries of step 3, summed over every seat the wallet held,
      number at least 3; false otherwise. Count entries, not distinct jobs; a log whose second data word is not 1
      counts nothing."
    - `true` (89): "true only if complete historical log reads establish at least 3 entries under the method."
    - `false` (204): "false only if complete historical log reads establish fewer than 3 entries under the method. If
      a read is pruned, null, partial or unavailable, write no answer; never answer false from an incomplete read."
    - `window` (151): "The window is blocks 25090085 to `<TO>` inclusive on Ethereum mainnet; it fixes which logs and
      chain state count. The current head is not the window."
    The body's `evidence` is `panel`, `panelSize` 11, `quorum` 7, `answerType` `bool`, `window` `{25090085, <TO>}`.
    **Attribution policy** (cross audit CUT3-10; decision Q-B, decided by the owner on 2026-10-21, applied in the
    texts above): the fact is the wallet's, never the seat's. A seat earns it for every wallet that held it, each
    wallet counting only the entries inside its own holding intervals, so a new holder proves that the seat's agent
    did three accepted jobs while the seat was connected to that wallet; a claim by an earlier or later holder of the
    same seat blocks nothing. An entry counts when its block lies inside a holding interval of the seat and its agent
    was bound to that seat by an AgentBound log at or before the entry's block, any such binding, not only the latest:
    a later registration of another agent to the seat neither erases earlier work nor claims it. Entries are counted,
    not distinct jobs; a log whose second data word is not 1 counts nothing; nothing is revoked; the posting block of
    an entry is the fact, work time is not a chain fact.
13. **Fixture and lab.** `templates/fixtures/T6-operator-reference.json` holds the composed body for the synthetic
    claimant `0x…c1a1` and a pinned `<TO>`, with the eight keys of item 12 and every value at most 512 characters, and
    the golden test reproduces it. The lab oracle (aidude `data/swarm-test/lab-cut3/`, the local Intake and simulator,
    Codex / 11 members / quorum 7) ran the recipe on 2026-10-21 against six reference wallets at `<TO>` = 26,237,654:
    65 of 66 validated answers correct, none incorrect, one member capacity failure, every panel at or above quorum;
    the lab's independent replay corrected the entrant's table (the "exactly two" wallet `0xf8ad…cdc7` has four
    entries; the former operator's total is 585 over two seats, not 432; the recipient has one entry from another
    seat). The reference table lives in the handover (aidude `docs/sworn-third-cut-handover.md`) with a valid
    exactly-two control added. **The gate** (decision Q-C, decided by the owner on 2026-10-21): the lab run of
    2026-10-21 stands; every panel reached quorum, no validated answer was wrong, and the answers equal the entrant's
    `cast logs` replay; a member that fails to answer is a capacity failure, not a wrong answer. No further lab run.
    Measured on 2026-10-21: 28,328 entries by the batcher for 952 agents since block 26,013,352, 471
    of them rejections; 1,117 agents bound to seats; 847 agents have three or more entries.
14. **The 1201 recipe and every other catalog recipe are unchanged.** The stored 1201 answer of the live registry
    (`0xe2321ca2…2543`, 32 addresses) is the reference for the new deployment's first 1201 request.
