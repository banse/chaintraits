## 1. Registry: the pool goes, every fee burns (default)

1. **No sponsor pool.** Remove `claimSponsored`, `sponsor`, `pool`, `poolUsed`, `lastPoolDay`, `sponsoredVerified`,
   `POOL_DRAW_CAP`, `MAX_SPONSORED_VERIFIED`, `Pending.sponsored`, `_release`, `_burnOrRefund`, `_refund`, `PREVIEW_GAS`,
   `BURN_GAS`, `BURN_MIN`, `BURN_FEE_CAP`, the errors `InsufficientGas`, `BurnRefundFailed`, `PoolDrawTooLarge`,
   `PoolAlreadyUsed`, `PoolLifetimeLimit`, `PoolDailyLimit`, `NotMember`, `NotACatalogChainTrait`, the events `Sponsored`,
   `PoolSpent`, `BurnTriggered`, `BurnSkipped`, and every `payable`. The Registry never holds IMD at rest and never handles
   ETH. (Closes audit F-2, F-4, F-11, F-14, F-16 and the whole M-1 family of the first audit.) **No reference remains:**
   the words "pool", "sponsor", "sponsored" and "donate" do not occur in `src/`, `test/`, `web/src/`, `web/public/`,
   `CATALOG.md`, `CONCEPT.md`, `INTEGRATION.md`, `TEMPLATES.md`, `README.md` or the site's copy (the owner, 2026-10-21);
   only `DEPLOYMENTS.md`, `audits/` and the `docs/` history keep them as the record of the earlier deployments.
   Acceptance: `grep -ril 'pool\|sponsor\|donat' src test web/src web/public CATALOG.md CONCEPT.md INTEGRATION.md TEMPLATES.md README.md`
   prints nothing.
2. **Every fee is burned.** `mintCard` moves `MINT_FEE` (0.1 IMD) and `registerSpec` moves `SPEC_FEE` (6.4 IMD) from the
   caller straight to BurnExecutor V2 (`0xe29386719C155B6847aD5a4E97C6674f10ffc750`), whose pending amount is its IMD
   balance; anyone bridges and burns it on Base by paying the LayerZero fee, as today. The constructor argument keeps its
   name `burnExecutor_`, must be nonzero and must have code (item 10). Oracle prices still go to the Intake. Tests: a mint
   raises the executor's IMD balance by exactly 0.1 IMD and leaves the Registry's at zero; `previewBridge()` on a fork
   reports the fee as pending.
3. **`abandon` on a shared fact is permissionless after `ABANDON_AFTER`** (audit F-3, the auditor's recommendation taken as
   written). The payer is no longer recorded; `Pending.payer` goes. A personal pair keeps its claimant-only `abandon`.
   Nothing is lost: the payer could already abandon after 31 days and still can, the fee was never refundable, and the
   stored-fact path is untouched; item 4's issue window makes the 31 days a guarantee (every attestation a request can
   ever receive is dead before anyone may abandon it). Test: a stranger's `abandon` of a shared fact at day 15 reverts
   `TooEarly`, at day 32 succeeds and clears the pair; the claimant's personal abandon at day 32 succeeds; a stranger's
   personal abandon at day 32 reverts.
4. **The attestation's dates are bounded** (audit F-7). `_check` adds `a.issuedAt >= p.requestedAt`,
   `a.issuedAt <= p.requestedAt + ISSUE_WINDOW` with `ISSUE_WINDOW = 1 days` (the plane answers in minutes; a day is
   generous) and `a.expiresAt <= a.issuedAt + Composer.VALID_FOR_SECONDS`; a miss is reason 7. With `ABANDON_AFTER =
   31 days` this restores the invariant the constant's comment claims: an attestation is dead before a pending pair can be
   abandoned, so a late valid answer can never collide with an abandoned request. Test: an attestation issued before the
   request, one issued two days after it, and one valid for 31 days each miss with 7 and clear the pair; one issued 23 hours
   after the request and valid for 30 days passes.
5. **A pending record is never overwritten** (audit F-10). `_request` reverts `RequestIdTaken(requestId)` when
   `_pending[requestId].requestedAt != 0`. Test: a mock Intake that returns a used id makes the request revert.
6. **Trait 601 "Swarmed" is the operator fact, sworn, not read.** The family is renamed from "Armed" to "Swarmed" (the owner, 2026-10-21): in the Renderer's name table, the metadata, `CATALOG.md`, `TEMPLATES.md`, the site and every document; the live deployment keeps "Armed" as it is. See section 2 for the recipe. In the Registry: 601 leaves the
   direct set (`directSnapshot` covers the 13 Curator-read ids, bit 20 is never set, `hasDirectTrait(wallet, 601)` is
   false and `frozenDirect` keeps its layout); `hasTrait(wallet, 601)` answers from `_verified` like every oracle id;
   `_rule(601)` returns kind 6, `fromBlock` = 25,090,085 (the IdentityMD collection's creation block), a **living**
   `toBlock` = the request's `block.number - 64`, threshold 3; `_panelOf(6)` is 11 / 7 and `_answerTypeOf(6)` is bool. The
   attestation is bound to the request's window: two requests of the same wallet at different blocks carry different
   question hashes, and `_check` compares the attestation's window with the pending request's, not with a constant.
   A `Missed` 601 can be requested again at once (more jobs later). 601 is a paid upgrade like every oracle id. Tests:
   the window in the composed body equals the request block minus 64; an attestation for an earlier window of the same
   wallet misses with 6 or 10; a true verdict lights 601 and `traitsOf` lists it; `hasDirectTrait(…, 601)` is false for
   every wallet.
7. **The IdentityMD dependency leaves the constructor.** `idmd_` and the `idmd` getter go from Registry and Composer
   (`directSnapshot(wallet, curator)`); the collection address becomes a constant of the operator recipe (item 12). The
   getter count drops to seven; `sworn-verify` expects seven.
8. **Kept from the last cut without change:** `uint16` community ids (registration ends at id 65,534), the `onResult`
   order (clear, then check), the kind-3 completeness rule, the 31-day `ABANDON_AFTER`, transferable cards with facts

9. **Removed documents in code:** the NatSpec of `claimSponsored`, `sponsor` and the burn rule; `INTEGRATION.md`'s pool

## 2. Composer: the operator recipe

10. **Kind 6, catalog-only.** A new question kind for id 601, not offered to community specs (`validateSpec` keeps
    kinds 1 to 5). Evidence class `panel` (archive state and logs across three contracts), panel 11 / quorum 7, answer
    type bool, `validForSeconds` as today.
11. **The question** (the exact wording is the builder's, the meaning is not): "Up to block `<TO>` on Ethereum mainnet,
    did the wallet `<A>` operate an IdentityMD seat through at least 3 accepted Identity.md swarm jobs while it held
    that seat? Answer true or false."
12. **definitions.method**, in this order, with these constants:
    1. *Seats held.* Every `Transfer(address indexed from, address indexed to, uint256 indexed tokenId)` log of the
       IdentityMD collection `0x0000eC93127BAA929E58E97dd0095A2BFb38ec1D` from block 25,090,085 to `<TO>` whose `from`
       or `to` is `<A>`. For each `tokenId`, the wallet's holding intervals: from the block of a transfer to `<A>`
       (inclusive) to the block of the next transfer of that token away from `<A>` (exclusive); an interval still open at
       `<TO>` ends at `<TO>` inclusive. Transfers in one block are ordered by log index.
    2. *The agent of each seat.* The `AgentBound(uint256 indexed agentId, uint8 indexed standard, address indexed
       tokenContract, uint256 tokenId, address registeredBy)` logs of the adapter `0xde152AfB7db5373F34876E1499fbD893A82dD336`
       with `tokenContract` equal to the collection and `tokenId` equal to the seat; the latest binding at or before `<TO>`.
       A seat without a binding has no agent and counts nothing.
    3. *Accepted jobs.* The `NewFeedback` logs (topic 0 `0x6a4a61743519c9d648a14e6493f47dbe3ff1aa29e7785c96c8326a205e58febc`)
       of the reputation registry `0x8004baa17c55a88189ae136b182e5fda19de9b63` whose topic 1 is the agent id, whose
       topic 2 is the batcher `0xb6d0a187b050fa5bb0b87033a203f37becf4a775`, whose second data word (bytes 32 to 63) equals 1,
       and whose block lies inside one of the wallet's holding intervals of that seat.
    4. *The answer.* true when the entries of step 3, summed over every seat, number at least 3; false otherwise. A
       pruned, null or partial log read, or an endpoint without the window, is no evidence: write no answer, never false.
    The texts of `definitions.true` and `definitions.false` follow the T5 pattern of `TEMPLATES.md`.
13. **Fixture and lab.** `templates/fixtures/T6-operator-reference.json` holds the composed body for a reference wallet and
    `<TO>`, with the expected answer and the log counts behind it. Before anything is paid, the lab oracle (aidude
    `data/swarm-test/lab-oracle-*`, the local Intake and simulator) runs the recipe for at least four wallets: one that
    operated a seat through many accepted jobs (true), one that received its seat after the seat's jobs (false), one with
    two accepted jobs (false), one with three entries of which one is a rejection (false); 11 of 11 members must agree
    on all four, and the answers must match a `cast logs` replay. Measured on 2026-10-21: 28,328 entries by the batcher
    for 952 agents since block 26,013,352, 471 of them rejections; 847 agents have three or more entries.
14. **The 1201 recipe and every other catalog recipe are unchanged.** The stored 1201 answer of the live registry
    (`0xe2321ca2…2543`, 32 addresses) is the reference for the new deployment's first 1201 request.
